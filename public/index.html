Bring in a Gmail account's most-watched and subscribed channels
  1. Open takeout.google.com signed in with that Gmail.
  2. Press Deselect all, then tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Press All YouTube data included and keep history and subscriptions ticked, then OK.
  4. Press Next step → Create export. Google emails a download link, usually within a few minutes.
  5. Download the zip and choose it below. VidGate reads it on this phone and ranks channels by how many of their videos were watched.
