YouTube channels

New videos from these channels come into "To review" automatically. VidGate checks them every hour, and whenever you press "Check all channels now".

Bring in every channel a Gmail account subscribes to
  1. Open takeout.google.com signed in with that Gmail.
  2. Press Deselect all, then tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Press All YouTube data included, keep only subscriptions, then OK.
  4. Press Next step → Create export. Google emails a download link, usually within a few minutes.
  5. Download the zip and choose it below (the zip itself, or the subscriptions.csv inside it).

    Results join the review queue. Each search uses 100 of the 10,000 daily YouTube API units.
